# Alpha Male Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 30, 2026

Alpha Male is operated by Hafiz Muhammad Zuhaib, using Synapse Stacks as the product and support brand ("Synapse Stacks," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when you use the Alpha Male mobile application, websites, backend services, support channels, and related features (collectively, the "Service").

Contact: contact@synapsestacks.com

Alpha Male is a private self-improvement and recovery app designed to help users reduce or avoid explicit content, masturbation/fapping habits, compulsive sexual behavior patterns, and related relapse cycles. Because of that purpose, information you choose to enter may be sensitive. Please read this policy carefully before using the Service.

## 1. Summary

- We collect account information, profile settings, recovery progress, check-ins, goals, journals/accountability notes, relapse and urge logs, Shield Mode settings, notification preferences, social posts/replies/reactions, app diagnostics, and support messages.
- Some information may reveal sensitive personal details, including sexual behavior, explicit-content use, urges, emotional state, stress, sleep, spending related to destructive content, and recovery goals.
- We use this information to run the app, calculate streaks and progress, provide reminders, power recovery tools, enable optional social/accountability features, improve reliability, provide support, and protect the Service.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not use sensitive recovery data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
- Alpha Chief, the in-app AI mentor, sends your chat messages and limited account context to our backend and AI infrastructure to generate a response. We do not persist full Alpha Chief chat transcripts as message logs in our database at this time, but we may log usage metadata.
- You can request account deletion in the app or by emailing contact@synapsestacks.com.

## 2. Who should use Alpha Male

The Service is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority in your location if higher, to use Alpha Male.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate deletion steps.

### 3.7 Alpha Chief AI mentor data

When you use Alpha Chief, we process:

At this time, Alpha Chief chat messages are not persisted by us as full chat-history records in our database. However, your messages are transmitted to our backend and AI infrastructure to generate responses. We may retain non-message metadata in analytics events to monitor cost, reliability, safety routing, and abuse.

### 3.8 Shield Mode and device-control data

Shield Mode is designed to add friction against selected apps or adult websites. Depending on platform and permissions, we may process:

- Whether Shield Mode is enabled.
- Whether app blocking or website blocking is enabled.
- Disable-delay preference.
- Native setup status, such as active, inactive, permission revoked, unsupported, or error.
- Ruleset/version status and sync timestamps.

On iOS, Shield Mode uses Apple's FamilyControls and ManagedSettings frameworks when available. The selected app, category, and web-domain tokens are stored locally on your device using Apple's APIs/UserDefaults and applied through iOS managed settings. Our server stores Shield Mode preference/status information, not the readable names of every selected app or website.

On Android, Shield Mode may use Accessibility permission to detect when a selected blocked app comes to the foreground, and VPN permission to route DNS lookups through a local DNS filtering service that forwards DNS requests to Cloudflare Family DNS (1.1.1.3). Android selected package names and VPN running status are stored locally on your device. We do not use Accessibility to read messages, passwords, form fields, or screen content.

You can turn Shield Mode off in the app or revoke permissions in your device settings.

At this stage, the core local reminders are Daily Pledge and Daily check-in. Some notification records may also support progress updates or future features.

## 8. Account deletion and returning users

You may request account deletion in the app or by contacting contact@synapsestacks.com.

When your account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize account-linked personal information from active systems, including profile data, private recovery records, check-ins, goals, journals/accountability notes, notification records, Shield Mode server preferences, and uploaded avatar records, unless retention is required or permitted for legal, safety, security, fraud-prevention, abuse-prevention, dispute-resolution, or accounting reasons.

For shared Alpha Tribe content, we may delete, hide, or anonymize posts/replies and remove profile identifiers. Some thread metadata may be retained where needed to preserve community integrity, enforce rules, or maintain moderation records.

We may retain a minimal deletion or security audit record, such as a timestamp and a non-reversible or keyed hash of your normalized email address or provider identifier. This is used only to honor deletion, prevent abuse, protect the Service, and recognize that an account tied to the identifier previously requested deletion. It is not used to restore your old profile, journals, relapse logs, check-ins, or recovery history. If you return after deletion, you should expect to start with a new account unless we explicitly offer a grace-window restoration flow.

Deletion from backups and logs may take additional time, but deleted data is not used for normal app operation after deletion is processed.
