## 14. Content Priorities for the Next UX/UI Design

The redesigned interface should preserve the following content priorities:

1. Make the company feel technically credible immediately.
2. Clearly communicate the agency's service breadth without making the page feel cluttered.
3. Keep the primary CTA visible and easy to act on.
4. Make services easy to scan.
5. Emphasize scale, reliability, AI, modernization, and engineering rigor.
6. Communicate that SynapseStacks can own complex technical work end-to-end.
7. Avoid sounding like a generic web design agency.
8. Preserve the feeling of an engineering partner, not a commodity freelancer shop.
